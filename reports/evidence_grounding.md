# Evidence-grounding benchmark

The deterministic evidence-grounding candidate was run on six authored controls: accuracy `0.50`. This is intentionally reported as a weak control result, not a production claim. The simple token-support method is insufficient for synonyms, contradiction, and multi-hop business evidence.

Amazon Contextual Product QA is registered as evaluation-only pending rights review and has not been downloaded. The current candidate therefore remains a safety gate and review aid, not an accepted learned module.
