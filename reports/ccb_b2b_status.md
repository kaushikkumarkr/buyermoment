# CCB-B2B status

The CCB-B2B schema and review queue are implemented. The current controlled silver foundation contains:

- Silver scenarios: `22`
- Human-reviewed records: `0`
- Gold holdout: `0`
- Pending review queue: `22`
- Categories: `11` of the planned initial category list
- Azure-generated records: `0`

The records are manually authored controlled scenarios with explicit provenance and a `SILVER` quality tier. They are not customer statements, public market prevalence, or B2B advertising outcomes. The Pydantic contract is `B2BCommercialContextRecord`; the seed and manifest are generated by `scripts/build_ccb_b2b_seed.py`.

The B2B gold benchmark is unavailable pending independent human review. The next safe step is to review a held-out subset without tuning on its labels.
