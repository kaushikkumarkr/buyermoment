# B2B service validation

The CCB-B2B silver queue was expanded from 22 to 44 controlled scenarios across 11 categories. It contains zero human-reviewed, gold, real-client, or real-campaign records. The queue is useful for schema and workflow validation only.

Three clearly labeled `DEMO_SYNTHETIC` service packages run through the service flow:

- CRM SaaS;
- Analytics/BI SaaS;
- Cybersecurity SaaS.

The demo flow produces Buyer Moments, OfferFit, LandingPageFit, Experiment Portfolio, measurement audit, and a client report. It does not establish market demand, ad performance, or ROI.
