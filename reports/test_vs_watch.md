# TEST versus WATCH

This is the dedicated manually authored Phase 5 readiness benchmark. Both labels are commercially plausible; `TEST` means immediate evidence is strong enough for a human-approved experiment, while `WATCH` means commercial potential exists but readiness is insufficient.

See `artifacts/test_readiness_benchmark.json` for every prediction, threshold candidate, and validation/hidden split. The selected policy was chosen on validation only with a minimum validation TEST precision of 0.90, then evaluated once on hidden examples.
