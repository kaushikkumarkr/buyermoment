# Phase 4 pilot gate

**Decision: NO-GO**

The gate is intentionally conservative. The Phase 4 adversarial v2 control set measures a 4.55% Waste-Risk Rate overall and 8.33% on its held-out half; held-out TEST precision is 50%. Purchase-stage accuracy is 56.67% on the difficult set. Counterfactual controls pass, but they are small and manually authored. The 45 demo-business candidates are not customer or revenue validation, and there are no human-reviewed gold labels for this phase.

Therefore BuyerMoment may produce evidence-backed hypotheses for internal review, but it must not influence live advertiser spend without explicit human approval and a future gate review.
