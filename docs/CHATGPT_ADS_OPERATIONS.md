# ChatGPT Ads operations

BuyerMoment prepares an experiment plan; it does not launch or autonomously modify campaigns.

1. Confirm the client owns the account and has approved the Buyer Moment, offer, landing page, budget, audience/context, and conversion event.
2. Review the generated campaign/ad-group/ad package against the current OpenAI Ads Manager workflow.
3. Treat context hints as natural contextual situations, not exact-match keywords or delivery commands.
4. Confirm title/copy/landing-page limits and the current documented bulk-upload template before upload.
5. Confirm UTMs, conversion tracking, privacy language, and client-approved data handling.
6. Manually upload/configure, perform policy review, and record the human approver.
7. Export/report outcomes with `experiment_id` and `buyer_moment_id` preserved.

No component claims access to private ChatGPT conversations. Platform reporting is aggregated and must be joined through experiment metadata and tracking parameters.
