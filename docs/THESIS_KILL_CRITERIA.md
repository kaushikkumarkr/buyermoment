# Thesis kill criteria

Record, do not hide, signals that challenge the thesis:

- Buyer Moments are generic or obvious and add no decision value;
- advertisers already derive equivalent insight without a workflow;
- context hypotheses cannot map to controllable platform experiments;
- users like reports but never accept or repeat experiments;
- evidence grounding remains unreliable after review;
- real outcomes show no useful relationship between Buyer Moment quality and qualified business outcomes;
- native platforms absorb the workflow without a durable advertiser-side need;
- willingness to pay or repeat usage does not emerge.

No pivot follows automatically from one negative signal. Review evidence across clients and cycles.
