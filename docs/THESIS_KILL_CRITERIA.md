# Thesis kill criteria

These are signals to record and review, not automatic pivot instructions:

- Buyer Moments are consistently generic or obvious.
- Advertisers already derive equivalent insight without the workflow.
- Context hints cannot be meaningfully translated into a reviewable experiment.
- Advertisers value reports but do not accept or revisit experiments.
- Recommendations do not map to targetable campaign structures.
- Real outcomes show no useful relationship to ContextFit or TestReadiness after adequate data.
- Native platforms absorb the workflow without a durable advertiser-side intelligence need.
- No design partners will share feedback, repeat usage, or willingness-to-pay signals.

Negative evidence must be preserved in `commercial_validation_status.md`. No product pivot happens automatically from one review or one small campaign.
