# Internal live experiment preparation

This document prepares, but does not authorize, a tiny BuyerMoment-on-BuyerMoment campaign.

Proposed shape:

- three BuyerMoment-derived hypotheses;
- one or two experiments selected by a human owner;
- proposed total cap: `$150–$200`, subject to separate explicit approval;
- manual campaign creation in the official platform UI;
- one primary conversion: `pilot_request`;
- static experiment and Buyer Moment tracking parameters;
- human review before launch, during the run, and before any change.

Stop immediately on budget exhaustion, tracking failure, landing-page failure, policy/review issue, clearly irrelevant traffic, unexpected billing, or a mismatch between the approved package and the live configuration.

This test is plumbing validation, not a claim of statistical superiority, ROAS improvement, CAC reduction, or platform outperformance. BuyerMoment may not autonomously launch, pause, bid, or move budget.
