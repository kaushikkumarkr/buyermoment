# Experimentation playbook

One Buyer Moment should map to one clear hypothesis and an immutable launch-time assessment. Change one major variable at a time where practical: context, offer, landing page, or creative. Record control/treatment, planned budget, stop-loss, tracking status, conversion lag, and uncertainty.

Allowed decisions are `SCALE`, `RETEST`, `MODIFY_OFFER`, `MODIFY_LANDING_PAGE`, `WATCH`, `STOP`, and `NEW_TEST`. Every decision requires evidence and human approval.
