# Service operating playbook

1. Qualify a B2B SaaS prospect and confirm the problem is commercial-context intelligence, not guaranteed ROAS.
2. Keep the client as account owner; obtain authorized least-privilege platform access.
3. Obtain approved website, product, pricing, customer, ad, CRM, and outcome data.
4. Create a client workspace and ingest evidence with source/provenance.
5. Review the BusinessProfile and unknowns.
6. Generate and deduplicate Buyer Moments.
7. Review evidence, ProductFit, Commercial Potential, OfferFit, LandingPageFit, TestReadiness, and SpendDecision.
8. Select a small Experiment Portfolio.
9. Prepare ChatGPT Ads and Google AI Max plans.
10. Run the Measurement Audit; repair unsafe tracking.
11. Obtain written human/client approval.
12. Manually configure and verify the approved platform experiment.
13. Import platform outcomes and CRM/pipeline outcomes.
14. Analyze qualified leads, pipeline, and revenue by Buyer Moment.
15. Choose SCALE/RETEST/MODIFY/STOP with evidence and uncertainty.
16. Generate the client report and refresh evidence for the next cycle.

No step authorizes autonomous launch, budget changes, bid changes, or payment actions.
