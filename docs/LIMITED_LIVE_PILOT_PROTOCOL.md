# Limited live pilot protocol (not executed)

This document is preparation only. Phase 6 does not authorize live advertising.

If a future gate explicitly permits a limited pilot, use no more than 1–3 businesses and one or two human-approved Buyer Moments per business. Freeze the hypothesis, landing page, audience/context hint, budget cap, success metric, and stop-loss before launch. A human selects and approves every budget and creative. No autonomous budget movement, bid changes, or campaign creation is permitted.

Record impressions, clicks, cost, conversions, conversion value, timestamps, and platform identifiers. Stop immediately on tracking failure, policy concern, serviceability mismatch, or spend above the approved cap. Compare realized economics with predicted ContextFit only after the campaign has ended; do not convert a short pilot into a performance claim.
