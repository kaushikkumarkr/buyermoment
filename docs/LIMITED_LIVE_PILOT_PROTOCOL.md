# Limited live pilot protocol (prepared, not launched)

This document is a future protocol only. No campaign is launched by this repository.

- 1–3 client-approved businesses;
- one or two approved Buyer Moments per business;
- small pre-approved total budget and daily cap;
- manual human launch and review;
- tracking-ready conversion event and experiment IDs;
- stop on tracking failure, policy issue, irrelevant traffic, unexpected billing, or budget cap;
- import impressions/clicks/spend/conversions plus CRM qualification and lag;
- do not claim statistical superiority or ROAS from a tiny sample.
