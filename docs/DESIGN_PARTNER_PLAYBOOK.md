# B2B SaaS design-partner playbook

## Who to target

Start with five B2B SaaS companies or agencies with a clear product, identifiable ICP, public or provided offer, an active website, and a decision maker who can review a marketing hypothesis. Do not represent outreach as a partnership until the company agrees.

## Proposition

> BuyerMoment identifies the customer situations your product is best positioned to win, ranks which are most worth advertising against, and turns the strongest into AI/search advertising experiments.

The initial pilot can be free. The purpose is to learn whether the evidence-to-experiment workflow is useful and repeatable, not to promise revenue or ROI.

## Required input

Company URL or product description, offer/landing page, target geography, ICP notes, and any authorized customer evidence. Website-only analysis is allowed but must mark unknowns. CRM, support, sales, and search-term files are optional and must be explicitly authorized.

## Workflow

1. Create a private business evidence package.
2. Mark every claim as observed, inference, hypothesis, or unknown.
3. Generate 15–30 raw moments; deduplicate and retain 8–15 review-worthy candidates.
4. Review evidence, ContextFit, Commercial Potential, TestReadiness, and SpendDecision.
5. Export a manual experiment package only after human approval.
6. Ask for structured feedback and record it separately from ML labels.
7. Import campaign outcomes only from an authorized export and retain lineage.

## Feedback questions

Ask whether the moment was known before, is commercially relevant, would be tested, has an appropriate offer, has credible evidence, is missing anything, would be used again, and would be paid for. Record `yes/maybe/no` plus the reviewer’s reason.
