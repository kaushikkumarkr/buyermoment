# Google AI Max operations

BuyerMoment supplies the commercial hypothesis and measurement requirements. Google supplies auction delivery, matching, and bidding.

For each Buyer Moment, review:

- AI Max suitability and whether Search conversion tracking is ready;
- search-intent themes and exclusions;
- final URL/landing-page strategy, URL controls, and brand controls;
- location and language settings;
- primary conversion and value signal;
- experiment/control design and conversion lag;
- CRM/offline conversion path for qualified pipeline.

This phase emits a planning JSON contract only. It does not implement OAuth, bid changes, campaign creation, or an undocumented Google API. Use official Google Ads documentation before every operational change.
