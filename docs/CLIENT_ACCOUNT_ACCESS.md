# Client account access

The client owns the advertising account, billing relationship, conversion data, and platform terms. BuyerMoment receives least-privilege authorized access; passwords and payment credentials are never requested or stored.

## ChatGPT Ads

Use the client's Ads Manager account and the currently documented manual or bulk-upload workflow. Export the BuyerMoment package for client review. The client/operator performs account selection, billing, policy review, upload, launch, and pause actions.

## Google Ads / Manager Account

Use client-granted manager/account access with the minimum role required for the task. Prefer exports and manual imports until an approved OAuth/API integration exists. Offline conversion uploads must use the client's approved CRM identifiers, consent, and retention policy.

Access must be time-bounded where possible, revocable, logged, and reviewed after the engagement. Human approval is mandatory for any external change.
